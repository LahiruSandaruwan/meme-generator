Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 31, 2025

TL;DR: Meme Generator is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect minimal information to provide and improve our service. Your memes are stored locally on your device only. We use third-party services (AdMob, Firebase, Sentry) that may collect anonymous data for analytics, advertising, and crash reporting.

1. Information We Collect

Meme Generator collects minimal information to provide and improve our service:

1.1 Memes You Create

  • All memes you create are stored locally on your device only
  • We do NOT upload or store your memes on our servers
  • Your creative content remains private and under your control

1.2 Usage Analytics

  • Anonymous usage data: Which features you use, how often, app performance metrics
  • No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected
  • Helps us understand how to improve the app

1.3 Device Information

  • Device type: iOS or Android model
  • OS version: To ensure compatibility
  • App version: For crash reporting and updates
  • Device identifiers: Anonymous ID for analytics (not linked to personal identity)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app's functionality
  • Improve user experience and fix bugs
  • Analyze app usage patterns to develop new features
  • Display relevant advertisements (see Advertising section below)
  • Monitor and resolve crashes and errors

3. Advertising

This app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data to personalize ads.

3.1 Data Collected by AdMob

  • Device identifiers: Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android)
  • IP address: For approximate location-based ads
  • Location data: Approximate location (city-level, not precise GPS)
  • Ad interaction data: Which ads you view or click

3.2 Your Ad Choices

You can control ad personalization:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → Disable for Meme Generator
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Premium Upgrade: Remove all ads by upgrading to premium ($4.99)

Learn more about Google's privacy practices: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Storage

  • All memes you create are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage (iOS) or SharedPreferences (Android)
  • We do NOT have access to your memes
  • Memes are NOT transmitted to our servers or any third-party
  • If you uninstall the app, all locally stored memes will be deleted
  • You can manually delete memes from the gallery at any time

5. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

5.1 Camera (Optional)

  • Why: To take photos for custom meme creation
  • When: Only when you tap "Take Photo" in the app
  • Stored: Photos remain on your device; we don't upload them

5.2 Photo Library (Required)

  • Why: To select images for custom memes and save created memes
  • Access: You choose which photos to use; we can't access all photos

5.3 Microphone (Optional)

  • Why: For voice-to-text feature to speak meme captions
  • When: Only when you activate the microphone button
  • Processing: Voice converted to text locally; audio not stored

5.4 Internet (Required)

  • Why: For ads, analytics, trending memes, and app updates
  • Note: Core meme creation works offline

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services:

6.1 Google AdMob

  • Purpose: Ad serving and monetization
  • Data collected: Device ID, IP address, ad interactions
  • Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6.2 Firebase Analytics

  • Purpose: Anonymous app usage analytics
  • Data collected: App events, screen views, device info
  • Privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6.3 Sentry

  • Purpose: Crash reporting and error tracking
  • Data collected: Error logs, device info, app version
  • Privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

6.4 Expo

  • Purpose: App development and deployment platform
  • Data collected: Build and deployment metadata
  • Privacy policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

  • If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us
  • We will promptly delete such information
  • No account registration is required to use the app

8. Data Security

We take data security seriously:

  • Local storage: Memes stored using secure platform APIs
  • Network security: All network connections use HTTPS encryption
  • Third-party services: Only use reputable, security-focused providers
  • No passwords: No account creation means no password vulnerabilities

Note: While we implement security best practices, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

If you're in the EU, UK, or California, you have additional rights:

9.1 Right to Access

  • Request a copy of data we have about you (very minimal, mostly anonymous analytics)

9.2 Right to Deletion

  • Request deletion of your data
  • Delete memes directly in the app's gallery
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data

9.3 Right to Opt-Out

  • Disable analytics in Settings
  • Opt out of personalized ads (see section 3.2)

9.4 Right to Portability

  • Export your memes at any time (share/save feature)

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@memegenapp.com

10. Data Retention

  • Local memes: Stored until you delete them or uninstall the app
  • Analytics data: Retained by Firebase for 14 months, then automatically deleted
  • Crash reports: Retained by Sentry for 90 days, then automatically deleted
  • Ad data: Retained by AdMob according to their policy (typically 18 months)

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have different data protection laws.

  • Firebase (USA): Complies with GDPR via Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Sentry (USA): GDPR-compliant data processing
  • AdMob (USA): Part of Google's global infrastructure

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted:

  • On this page (with updated "Last Updated" date)
  • In the app (notification for significant changes)
  • Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices:

  • Email: support@memegenapp.com
  • Privacy inquiries: privacy@memegenapp.com
  • Response time: Within 48 hours

Summary: We respect your privacy. Your memes stay on your device. We use minimal anonymous data to improve the app. Third-party services (ads, analytics) may collect additional data as described above. You have full control over your data and can delete it anytime.
